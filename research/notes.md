# Source and evidence boundaries

Inspiration: Ideabrowser, September 7, 2026, daily idea **“Turn instruments into plugins that sell.”** [Publisher](https://www.ideabrowser.com/).

The source proposes collaborating with skilled players of niche instruments, recording their note range and selling sample libraries/plugins with a musician royalty. This repository is an independent implementation of that summarized concept; it does not reproduce the newsletter.

A direct public idea-page URL was not available in the extracted message, and a targeted public search did not locate it. Accordingly, no paywalled detail, market score, verified demand count or public idea-page pricing is claimed. Newsletter tracking and unsubscribe links are deliberately excluded.

Reedroom, its demo musician identities, catalog specifications, prices, art and royalty defaults are prototype choices. They are not facts about real artists or products. The audio is synthesized reference tones. No proprietary recordings were downloaded or redistributed.
